## Map pins

- When entering a new room, map pins for Arch objects and money bags should appear in the minimap.
- When returning to the safehouse, map pins for Arch objects and money bags (not in the same room) should remain on the
  minimap.
- When acquiring an Arch object, the map pin should disappear, even after returning to the safe house.
- When acquiring an Arch object, the map pin should disappear, even after quitting to the main menu.
- When acquiring an Arch object, the map pin should disappear, even when dying during the acquisition.
- When acquiring a money bag, the map pin should disappear, even after quitting or returning to the safe house.
- When acquiring a money bag, the map pin should disappear, even after returning to the safe house.
- When acquiring a money bag, the map pin should disappear, even after quitting to the main menu.
- When acquiring a money bag, the map pin should disappear, even when dying during the acquisition.

## Faria boss

- When starting the game, the dungeon's Mega-Battery map pin is replaced with a skull icon.
- When entering the boss arena with the Mega-Battery acquired, the boss should be present and the Mega-Battery holder is
  intact.
- After defeating the boss and not breaking the Mega-Battery holder, the player is prevented from leaving the arena.
- When breaking the Mega-Battery holder, the text on-screen shows the item name and player recipient.
- After breaking the Mega-Battery holder, the player can leave the arena.
- After defeating the boss, the slime NPC guarding the dungeon should be gone.
- After defeating the boss, the dungeon's map pin is updated with a check mark.

- When entering the boss arena with the Mega-Battery location already checked, the boss should still be present, but the
  Mega-Battery holder is broken.
- After defeating the boss with the Mega-Battery location already checked, the player can leave the arena.
